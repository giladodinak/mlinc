News Articles Corpus

The news dataset is available for download at
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sbhatti/news-articles-corpus
(see getnewsdata.sh in scripts directory)

The news dataset contains 870521 files (all_files.lst). Some are duplicates.
There are 580014 unique files, however some are not news articles.
Files containing 50 to 800 words seem to be news articles (selected_files.lst).
These 421592 files are split into training (80%), validation (10%), and
test (10%) files, respectively in tr_files.lst, vd_files.lst and te_files.lst.
